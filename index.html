FAQ

Questions you might have.

Do I need proof?

No. We believe you.

What if I don't have a ghost yet?

Open the haunt map. Lonely ghosts nearby are looking for a calm home. Adopt one, or foster for a week first.

Is my ghost dangerous?

Most are just lonely. If something feels off, like headaches or weeks of bad sleep, the app checks the house first and points you to real help.

What if I move house?

Some ghosts come with you, some stay with the house. Your profile, diary and rituals move with you either way.

Is it free?

Free to start. Ghostly+ is €4.99 a month for chat, Ghost Finder and all rituals.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your diary stays yours, and homes on the haunt map are anonymised.
